# Demonstration versus a regulated healthcare system

MedMLOps has no intended diagnostic, treatment or patient-management use. Its dataset, history and event are fictitious. Scores must never become medical recommendations. Synthetic accuracy is not clinical validation. No certification, authorization or compliance is asserted.

Relevant references for a future genuinely clinical product:

- **GDPR / RGPD**: evaluate lawful processing, data protection and safeguards whenever personal data are processed. Fictitious MVP records must not be replaced with actual health records. [Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj).
- **ISO 14971**: medical-device risk management process, distinct from ML metric gates. This project has no certified risk-management system. [ISO 14971:2019 official scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/72704.html).
- **IEC 62304**: medical-device software lifecycle processes. A tested repository does not demonstrate conformity to those processes or final device validation. [IEC 62304 official scope](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/6792).
- **EU MDR**: qualification and obligations depend on the actual product and intended purpose. The demo label alone cannot establish the status of a future clinical product. [Regulation (EU) 2017/745](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex%3A32017R0745).
- **EU AI Act**: applicability and system risk classification require analysis of the concrete system, roles and use case. No automatic classification or compliance claim is made here. [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj).

Future clinical deployment would require qualified clinical, quality, privacy and regulatory review; representative and independently validated datasets; patient-level/temporal/external evaluation; clinical evidence; risk controls; controlled change and post-market processes as applicable. These are gaps, not completed MVP capabilities.
